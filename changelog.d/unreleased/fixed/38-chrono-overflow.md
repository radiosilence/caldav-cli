- **Out-of-range dates and durations are errors, not crashes.** A huge `days`, relative offset or `durationMinutes`, or a server event with an enormous `DURATION`, overflowed chrono and panicked the request; the server-supplied case did so on every read of its calendar. Such values are now refused, or treated as zero-length when they come from the server. Times outside years 1–9999, which iCalendar cannot write, are refused rather than reading back as an empty result.
