- **Recurring events can no longer stall a read.** Expansion stepped through every recurrence since a series began, so one old `FREQ=SECONDLY` invite took minutes to read. Every series now has a step budget, and a sub-daily series starts near the window. A window that expands to more than 25,000 occurrences is an error rather than an oversized response. An all-day series with a date `UNTIL` expands again instead of showing only its first event, and `conflicts` no longer misses clashes on a day with more than 100 events.
